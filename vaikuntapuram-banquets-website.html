A word that means heaven, and a hall that tries to live up to it.

Vaikuntapuram borrows its name from Vaikuntha, the heavenly abode in Hindu tradition — and the hall was built to match the ambition of that name: tall ceilings, warm light, and enough floor space that no one in a large joint family gets left standing at the back.

Since opening in Kondapur in 2022, it's grown into one of the area's regular venues for weddings, milestone birthdays, griha pravesams and the kind of big, multi-generation family functions that need a hall built specifically for them — with catering handled in-house from the same kitchen, start to finish.

Occasions we build the day around

Every hall booking comes with catering from our own kitchen, so the food and the room are planned together, not stitched on separately.

Weddings & receptions

Halls laid out with room to spare for baraat, bidaai and everything the guest list needs in between.

Birthdays

From a first birthday to a sixtieth shanti — decor and a buffet line that suit either end of that range.

Anniversaries

A quiet dinner for twenty or a full renewal-of-vows evening for two hundred, same attention either way.

Family functions

Cultural gatherings

Festival lunches and community get-togethers, with a stage and sound set up when the occasion calls for one.
